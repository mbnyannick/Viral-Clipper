# Privacy Policy for VIRAL Bot

**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

VIRAL Bot ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Telegram bot and connected YouTube publishing services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

When you interact with VIRAL Bot, we collect minimal necessary information required to operate our service:

- **Telegram User ID & Chat ID**: Used to identify your session and deliver generated video clips.
- **Google YouTube OAuth Credentials**: When you explicitly authorize VIRAL Bot to publish YouTube Shorts to your personal YouTube channel, we receive an encrypted OAuth refresh token from Google.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use your information for the following purposes:

- **Video Processing & Delivery**: To generate, composite, and deliver short-form video clips requested by you.
- **Direct YouTube Publishing**: To upload requested video clips directly to your connected YouTube Channel using the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload` scope.
- **Account Identification**: To ensure your clips are uploaded only to your authorized YouTube channel.

We **NEVER** sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data or YouTube credentials with third parties.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Protection

- Your OAuth tokens are securely stored in protected configuration files on our private servers.
- We do not access, view, or modify any other YouTube channel settings, personal videos, playlists, or channel content outside of publishing the specific short clips you select.

---

## 4. User Control & Data Revocation

You retain full control over your connected YouTube account at all times:

- **Disconnect from Bot**: You can disconnect your channel at any time by sending `/disconnect` in Telegram.
- **Revoke Google Access**: You can revoke VIRAL Bot's permissions at any time via your [Google Security Account Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

---

## 5. Compliance with Google API Disclosure

VIRAL Bot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
